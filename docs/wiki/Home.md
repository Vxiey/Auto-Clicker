## VxClick 1.0.0

Version 1.0.0 includes the precision clicker, global hotkeys, native macro recording and playback, key/mouse remapping, Lua automation, process-aware profiles, diagnostics and the Windows updater/release workflow.

VxClick does not include anti-cheat bypass or anti-detection functionality.
